## The Challenge

Mombasa Millers faced significant inventory spoilage due to inaccurate demand forecasting and poor environment monitoring in their silos.

## The Solution

We deployed **Audit-Vision** combined with AI-driven demand forecasting n8n workflows to monitor silo conditions and predict order volumes.

## The Result

Stockouts were reduced by 40% and overall production waste dropped by 22%, increasing annual net profit margins by 4.2%.
