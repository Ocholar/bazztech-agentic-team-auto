## The Challenge

Nile Foods had a fragmented data landscape, where production logs were manually entered into their ERP, causing 12.5% data entry errors.

## The Solution

By deploying **Bazz-Flow (ERP-Bridge)**, we unified their shop floor data with SAP in real-time, eliminating manual entry.

## The Result

Data accuracy reached 99.8%, and the automated audit-ready reports allowed Nile Foods to pass their ISO recertification in record time.
