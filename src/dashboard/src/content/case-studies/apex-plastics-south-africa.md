## The Challenge

Apex Plastics was struggling with aging machinery that frequently failed without warning, leading to missed delivery deadines and high scrap rates.

## The Solution

We implemented **Bazz-Connect (Equipment-Telemetry)** across 12 injection molding lines, capturing vibrations and temperature data every 10ms.

## The Result

Within 3 months, BazzAI's predictive maintenance alerts identified 6 critical bearing failures before they occurred, saving an estimated $45,000 in lost production time.
